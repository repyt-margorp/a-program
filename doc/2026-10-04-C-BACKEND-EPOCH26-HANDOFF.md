### Assessment

Agent adopted the target guard because complete finite-value validators already
exist and the positive/refusal/resource controls justify their use in the existing
all-child graph contract. Records copy by value, Self pointers borrow nodes; no
new ownership policy. Readable initialized immutable caller nodes and borrowed
lifetime/nonoverlap remain preconditions; validators do not authenticate arbitrary
pointers. Validation scratch is O(V), quadratic worst-case identity search, freed
on every exit; arena capacity covers result nodes separately. Source computation
depth256 remains, while identity may validate/borrow deeper finite inputs. Shared
subtrees preserve source recursion; no computation memoization/cost claim.

Ten new checked/trusted refusal pairs stay explicit: demanded effect, callback,
non-branching non-List record shape, three Self fields, recursive aggregate,
callable recursive field, indexed shape, missing Packet, later Packet and missing
enum. No recursive aggregate, dynamic/callable/indexed Acc/QuickSort, higher
Identity, general effect or owned graph completion. Actual Acc needs a source-
semantic applicability/erasure decision; existing views already expose the barrier.
Route through Merge/sole Job owner, without asserting a producer defect or extending
producer/schema/checker. Action notice already sent; no source owner edits.
